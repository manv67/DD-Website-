DUBAI TRANSPORT

Welcome to Dubai

Dubai’s public transportation network stands as a global benchmark for urban mobility. The system is designed to handle the desert climate, ensuring that every journey is cool, punctual, and efficient.

At its heart is the Dubai Metro, a fully automated, driverless system. This is complemented by an extensive fleet of modern buses and water taxis, all interconnected through the unified Nol Card system.

Metro Network

Dubai’s current metro network is a world-class system featuring the Red Line, which connects major landmarks like the Burj Khalifa and Dubai Marina, and the Green Line, which serves the city's historic cultural hubs.

As of 2026, the network is undergoing its most significant expansion yet with the Blue Line.
